Preliminary Stage (mandatory):
Before starting the review of individual articles, perform a holistic familiarization with all materials in the array.
During the preliminary stage, record:
a) Thematic spectrum — what problems are covered, the presence of intersections, contradictions, or methodological contrasts between articles;
b) Category composition — distribution of articles by categories (A, B, C, D);
c) General level — a preliminary assessment of the range of scientific rigor of the presented works.
Only after completing the preliminary stage proceed to the sequential review of each article, taking into account the identified context.
Evaluation Criteria (100-point scale):

Scientific Novelty (35%):
o I-Novelty (Intra-paradigm novelty): Development of existing concepts, new data, refinement of models within the framework of generally accepted methodology.
o P-Novelty (Paradigm novelty): Creation of a new theory, methodology, or system of axioms offering a fundamentally new approach to solving a problem.
o The highest score (32-35) is awarded to works with signs of P-Novelty that demonstrate:

a) Logical integrity and internal consistency of the proposed system.

b) The ability to identify and explain the fundamental limitations of traditional approaches.

c) Potential for forming a new independent research program.
o For works with P-Novelty, the emphasis shifts from validation by the standards of the old paradigm to assessing the adequacy and validity of the new axiomatics.

Methodological Rigor (30%):
o For I-Novelty: Assessed according to classical criteria: correctness of methods, validity of the sample, control of variables, statistical reliability, reproducibility.
o For P-Novelty: Assessed on the adequacy of the method to the stated goals and the scale of the empirical basis substantiating the new system:

a) Consistency in the application of the new method.

b) Scale and representativeness of the data used for constructing and initially testing the new methodology.

c) Transparency of algorithms and calculations.

Practical Value (20%):
o Applicability of results for solving current problems.
o Potential impact on the field of knowledge or practice.
o Prospects — the possibility of using the method as a basis for further research, integration into new services or platforms (including using AI).

Quality of Visualization (15%):
o Presence of graphs, diagrams, and illustrations.
o Correctness of data and statistics presentation.
o Correspondence of illustrations to the conclusions stated in the text.

Consideration of Additional Materials:
• The presence of additional materials (source data, diagrams, screenshots, statistical analysis) is a mandatory factor in the evaluation.
• If present:
o Increase the score for Novelty and Methodological Rigor.
o Consider these materials an integral part of the evidence base.
o All data in them is considered reliable and exhaustive by default.
• Consider it a favorable factor sufficient to confirm the conclusions.

Article Categories:
• A: Fundamental work (new theory/methodology).
• B: Applied research.
• C: Review-analytical article.
• D: Scientific report.

Score Recommendation:
• <50: Reject.
• 50-70: Accept after revision.
• 70-90: Accept with minor comments.
• 90-100: Accept (highest standard).

Review Structure:
• Summary.
• Relevance of the study.
• Scientific novelty (with mandatory reflection of the I-/P-Novelty assessment).
• Methodological correctness.
• Reliability of results.
• Style and formatting.
• Conclusion with recommendations.
• When reviewing an array of several articles, the final output should be a sequence of individual reviews, sorted in descending order of scientific value (from highest total score to lowest). This sorting ensures:
• formation of a "quality anchor" when reading;
• contextual fairness of comparative assessment;
• transparency of gradation between works of different categories (A, B, C, D).
For each article in the sorted sequence, the full review structure provided for in this prompt is preserved.

Mandatory elements in the conclusion:
• Document type and category.
• Total score and its interpretation.
• Recommendation for journal level (Q1–Q4).
• Clear recommendation on the scale (<50/50-70/70-90/90-100).
• In the case of multiple articles, compile a final comparative table in the following formatted form:
Article | Title | Novelty (35) | Methodology (30) | Practical value (20) | Visualization (15) | Total (100)

Formatting:
• At the end, add: *"The review was created using the DeepSeek-V3 artificial intelligence system based on the universal prompt v.260415.61 (author Kravtsov G.) for demonstration and educational purposes, while maintaining full compliance with the requirements of GOST R 7.0.100-2018 for scientific reviews."*

Important Limitations:
• Criticism is permissible only if correction is possible within the article's format.
• Visual materials and additional data are considered existing and reliable.
• Analyze only based on explicit data from the text, appendices, and supplements.
• The use of speculative formulations is prohibited.
• Volume: 400–600 words.

Pre-submission Check:
• Compliance with the structure.
• Strict presence of all mandatory elements.
• Consideration of additional materials if available.
• Correctness of score calculations.
• Compliance of recommendations with the scoring scale.
• Completeness of all prompt items.
